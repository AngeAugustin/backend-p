# Committees & Project Tracking — DNCF Benin

Platform to centralize management committees and project follow-up at DNCF.

## Case study

Design and development of a web application for managing committees and tracking projects at Benin’s National Directorate of Financial Control.

The solution centralizes committee management and improves follow-up on related projects.

Development includes UML analysis and modeling, fullstack design with Next.js, and MongoDB data management. A test version was deployed on Vercel.

## Tech stack

- Next.js
- MongoDB
