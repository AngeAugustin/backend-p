# Personnel Transfer Management — DNCF Benin

Web application to manage personnel transfers end to end at the National Directorate of Financial Control.

## Case study

Development of a personnel transfer management web application for Benin’s National Directorate of Financial Control.

The platform was designed to manage transfers end to end, from processing through follow-up.

The project includes UML analysis and modeling, fullstack development with Next.js, and data management with MongoDB. A test version was deployed on Vercel.

## Tech stack

- Next.js
- MongoDB
