# GRC SaaS Landing Page — Govern Group

Marketing site for the GRC SaaS presenting features, roadmap, and an AI conversational assistant.

## Case study

Design and development of a professional landing page for a Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) SaaS.

The marketing site presents the SaaS, its main features, and its roadmap, with a clear and structured product narrative.

Built with Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Shadcn UI, including an AI conversational assistant. A test version was deployed on Vercel.

## Tech stack

- Vite
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Shadcn UI
- Intelligence Artificielle
