# Governance, Risk & Compliance SaaS — Govern Group

GRC SaaS covering standards, ECOWAS-CEMAC obligations, AML/CFT, and risk management, with AI assistance.

## Case study

Development of a Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) SaaS to help companies manage governance, risks, and regulatory obligations.

The platform covers standards, ECOWAS-CEMAC regulatory obligations, anti-corruption, AML/CFT/PF, and risk management.

Frontend built with Vite/TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Shadcn UI, plus AI features for guidance, assistance, recommendations, and automation.

## Tech stack

- Vite
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Shadcn UI
- Intelligence Artificielle
