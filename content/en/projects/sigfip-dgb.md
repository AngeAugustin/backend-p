# SIGFIP Project Contribution — DGB Benin / GIZ

Contribution to the DGB scope redesign of SIGFIP, with a focus on interoperability and X-ROAD.

## Case study

Contribution to the SIGFIP project at Benin’s General Directorate of Budget, as part of redesigning the DGB scope of the Public Finance Management Information System.

The project aims to redigitize the information system and improve interoperability with the Ministry of Living Environment.

Technologies include Java 8u202, WildFly, MyBatis, SpringMVC, Log4j, and Oracle 12, with interoperability through X-ROAD.
